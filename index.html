How it works

In the world of perfumery it's hard to remember which scents you tried and liked. I built a simple system: spray a blotter, snap a photo of the bottle, pick a pocket, and the app tells you how many times to fold the strip so nothing gets mixed up. Later you just match the strip to the photo. Go sniff and enjoy!

Snap a photo of the bottle.
Pick a pocket.
Fold the strip the number of times the app shows.
At home, match the strip to the photo and save your favorites.
